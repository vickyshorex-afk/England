Places to Discover

Six remarkable destinations across the country

📍 Wiltshire

Stonehenge

One of the world's most famous prehistoric monuments, Stonehenge is a ring of massive standing stones erected some 4,500 years ago. Its purpose remains one of history's great mysteries.

📍South East England

Oxford

Why it's Beautiful: Known as the "City of Dreaming Spires," it boasts stunning, honey-colored university architecture, scenic rivers, and historic green spaces. Best for Tourists: Touring prestigious colleges like Christ Church (a Harry Potter filming location), punting on the River Cherwell, and exploring the Bodleian Library, say Happy to Wander and The Wanderful Me.

📍South West

Dorset Coast

Why it's Beautiful: A National Park featuring dramatic mountains, deep glacial lakes, and charming villages, making it ideal for outdoor enthusiasts

📍 London region

London

London is the capital of the UK, founded by Romans in AD 43 as "Londinium". It is one of the world's most populous and diverse cities (9 million+ people, 300+ languages). 📍The county of Cumbria in the North West of England

The Lake District

The Lake District, located in Cumbria, Northwest England, is a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its stunning glaciated ribbon lakes, rugged fell mountains, and literary connections. As a premier national park, it attracts visitors with activities like hiking, boating, and exploring villages such as Ambleside and Keswick.

📍North East England

Northumberland

Northumberland Coast: Features stunning, rugged coastlines, and historic sites such as Bamburgh Castle, which sits high above the sea, offering dramatic scenery and rich history.
